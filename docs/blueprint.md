# **App Name**: WorkWise

## Core Features:

- Staff Roster: Display a list of staff members with their IDs, names, and associated calendar IDs, pulled from the Staff sheet. Also manages each staffs work schedule, and maps their respective calendars to individual color keys.
- Customer Directory: Provide a searchable customer directory sourced from the Customer sheet, including customer ID, name, and address.
- Scheduling Interface: Enable administrators to view, add, modify, and delete work schedules, sourced from the main schedule tracking sheet, with the ability to assign staff and locations.
- Automated Geocoding: When a customer address is inputed for scheduling purposes, automatically attempt to add Latitudinal and Longitudinal coordinates to a jobsite if the jobsite does not currently have coordinates.
- Schedule Automation: Automatically update staff Google Calendars with work schedules, with calendar invites automatically generated for each schedule.
- Real-time status update: Display a list of employees and the actions which they perform during a normal work day: Arrive, begin task, finish task, depart. Use Google Maps to find the distance of an employee from a worksite using a tool
- Efficient Routing Tool: Calculate and display the optimal route between work locations and estimate travel times.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Vivid blue (#4285F4), inspired by Google Calendar's brand, will bring to mind efficient and businesslike actions. Its relatively high saturation creates a feeling of confidence without overwhelming users.
- Background color: Light blue-gray (#F1F3F4), is chosen as it complements the primary blue, providing a clean, professional backdrop without causing distractions. This creates a harmonious and comfortable reading experience, making the information presented more accessible.
- Accent color: Sky blue (#80DEEA), will be used for highlighting interactive elements and key actions.
- Font: 'Inter' (sans-serif), will be used as it provides a clean and modern look suitable for both headlines and body text, and ensures legibility across various screen sizes.
- Use a set of consistent, professional icons for navigation and actions.
- Maintain a clean and intuitive layout, making key information easily accessible.
- Implement subtle transitions and animations to enhance user experience without being intrusive.
